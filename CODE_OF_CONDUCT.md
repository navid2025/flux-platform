# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to each other. Discuss the code, not the person.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are about information, not intent.
- Critique the change, not the author. "This loses the previous value" beats "you broke it".
- Ask questions when something is unclear rather than guessing at motives.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change, and that this is not a judgement of you.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, insults, or sustained disruption of discussion.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any space where someone is
representing the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainer through the contact details on their GitHub profile.
Reports are handled privately. The maintainer may remove comments, close threads, or block
accounts as needed.

## Attribution

This is a short, plain-language code of conduct rather than an adaptation of a longer
template. If the project grows, adopting the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) would be a reasonable step.
